Strands and garments are held only with the new optional rig data: `strands[].reach` (how far a held strand's tip may be pulled from where it hangs, px) and `cloth[].reach` (how far a held hem may be pulled, px, never beyond the garment's `max`). Without it `canGrab` is false and `grab` returns null for that strand or garment, so a client falls back to the drag impulse of avatars built before these fields, which therefore keep their old gestures. While a strand is held its offset is bounded by its reach instead of its sway limit `max`, and after the release that allowance fades while the strand springs back. Avatars without the fields render and sway exactly as before. `tools/build-layers.py --strand-layers` cuts, for each strand with a reach, its lock (`strand_<i>`) and the base round it with the lock filled in smoothly from the pixels round it that are not hair (`strand_<i>_under`), named in `strands` of `layers.json`; the runtime draws them only while that strand is held and springs back (the plate in place of the base there, the lock over it as one piece), so a held lock moves without stretching the base. Without these layers a held strand bends the base as before.
